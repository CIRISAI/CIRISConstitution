# Changelog

The CIRIS Constitution is one document with one version line. Each release below is the text as it stood at the cut; the Parts state the rules as they now stand, and this file records what each edition changed.

## 1.0-rc19 (open)

- Opened 2026-11-10 from the rc18 tag. This edition re-cuts the decimals on the state processor's spine: the kernel stated once, the family model as data, then mechanism and stewardship. The long-term-support text stays 1.0-rc18 on the decimals it has, takes its own point editions, and each of those is carried into this edition by the map from the old decimals to the new.
- A Wise Authority's seat over one party's deferrals is conferred by that party in a signed row (4.3): a person for themselves, an agent's steward for the agent and never the agent's own key, to a person, for every domain or for one. The seated person may hand the answering to an agent, a domain seat may require a licence, an approval of an identity amendment is not handed on, and the rows from deferral to ruling to act read as one chain. One register row, staged. The transitions table now takes a family's signer rule from the text where the code enforces none, so a deferral response opens to a seated key and a deferral request to its own signer; the other families with no enforced signer rule remain listed as gaps.
- The family model names who may sign each family's rows wherever the text says so. Thirty-three families that had no signer rule have one, by stage where the text gives a stage its own signer, and the transition cells the text states are carried beside them. Five cells stay open, each pinned with the question that closes it: who states a duty, who signs an occurrence revocation, and how far a retirement row, a seal-opening row and a release order travel. The role check fails on an open cell that is not pinned, on a pin that has closed, and on a reserved family any registered key could write to. The story walks 96 steps, 28 of them refusals, and the fold check holds that a refusal moves nothing. The transitions manifest and the story trace gain members; consumers re-pin.
- The family model's default row is data (`formal/derivation/family_default_row.json`): one value in each of nine columns, held to be the commonest of its column. Each family lists the columns in which it departs, and `manifests/family_departures.tsv` carries the departures alone. 106 of 192 families are the default row in all nine columns. No wire change.
- Families that differ only in their final segment are one row with a closed vocabulary in that segment. `accord:lifecycle:{kind}` holds `active` and `confirmed`; `capacity:{factor}` holds the five capacity factors; `detection:{detector}` holds `cross_agent_divergence`, `temporal_drift` and `conscience_override_rate`; and `substrate_building` is a `{subject}` value of `credits:{domain}:{language}:{subject}`. The registry goes from 192 families to 184. No wire string changes: every dimension that resolved resolves to the folded row with the same polarity, reservation and version rule, and every refused dimension is refused. An unlisted word under `capacity:`, `detection:` or `accord:lifecycle:` is refused with `namespace_vocab_value_unregistered` where it was refused with `namespace_family_unregistered`; an unlisted word in a malformed spelling under `capacity:` or `detection:` is refused with `namespace_dimension_case_malformed`, and `detection:correlated_action` with a version and no axis is refused as an unlisted detector where it was refused with `missing_version_segment`. A folded row carries the lower kind confidence of its members. The match vectors name the folded rows and carry every listed value of a closed vocabulary; the registry, the family model, the transitions and the story trace are regenerated, and consumers re-pin.
