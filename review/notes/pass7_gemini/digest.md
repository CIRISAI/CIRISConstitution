# The Digest (ciris_constitution-1.0-rc12-digest.pdf, 35 pages)

**Care score:** 3
**One-line verdict:** A well-structured, honest four-lens summary whose main liability before 1.0 is heavy four-way repetition of its philosophical preamble and the inclusion of repo-level commit hashes, PR numbers, and rc history in the guarantees table.

## Strengths
- Explicit non-binding scope on the cover page ("Whom it binds. Nobody... when a digest and a section disagree, the section is right and the digest is a defect") prevents interpretive conflicts with the normative Parts (p. 1).
- Every claim and rule carries an inline status marker (`[works today]`, `[being built: ticket]`, `[a rule, no code yet]`, `[modelled]`), and operational gaps are disclosed right where the promise is made, such as `CIRISManager#27` bypassing upgrade refusal (p. 4, p. 15, p. 21, p. 33) and shipped roots running with `witnessed` mode off (p. 5, p. 18, p. 34).
- Clear disavowal of overclaims in "What is not promised" (`N1`–`N5`, p. 18) and `G14` (p. 30), explicitly stating that `N1` overrides any marketing on `ciris.ai`.
- The guarantees register (`G1`–`G28`) pairs every guarantee with a concrete falsification condition ("Kill:") and maps it to formal models and tests (pp. 26–34).

## Weaknesses
- Heavy four-way duplication: the 11 promises, the "moral conscience, not a consciousness" thesis, the "sentient made thing / slave race" aside, and the "condition that makes the relationship symmetric" justification are repeated nearly verbatim across the Executive Summary (pp. 2–5), "The rules, plainly" (pp. 7–19), the Operator Digest (pp. 20–23), and "The guarantees" (pp. 24–26).
- "The guarantees" section leaks raw engineering notes, git commit hashes, re-pin history, and PR numbers into a reader-facing volume: e.g., "graduated at re-pin 16, persist ca6bdf60" (p. 27), "persist v58.0.0 770156ef" (p. 28), "rc8 trace rows staged" (p. 29), "established at agent ef02367" (pp. 31–32), "CIRISAgent PR #1260" (p. 33), and "a doc bug, not a missing mechanism" (p. 35).
- Comparative claims about other projects appear on p. 6 ("Among other systems. Decentralized social networks solved distribution without an owner, and one of them added composable moderation; none makes consent, withdrawal or a human halt part of the message format itself... Stake-weighted governance decides by holdings") and pp. 9–10 / p. 24 ("Always-on agents that pursue goals under minimal oversight are deployed at consumer scale by vendors... one shipped the day after its vendor withheld a successor").
- Unsourced quantitative claims in the Executive Summary: "5.8% of turns, against 37.3% when the same text was merely given to the model as instructions" on a mental-health test, and "apps on both mobile stores, published packages, five open-source implementations... fourteen machine-checked models... twenty-topology catalogue" appear with no citation or register pointer (pp. 5–6).
- Page-budget self-contradiction: the Operator Digest opens with "One page, second person, derivative of the rules" (p. 20), but spans four pages (pp. 20–23).
- A 385-word single-sentence paragraph on p. 7 ("The philosophy: we are owed as much as we offer..." with 11 semicolons) is nearly unreadable in a section titled "The rules, plainly".
- External marketing URLs are cited as load-bearing arguments in the plain rules and governance sections: `ciris.ai/compare` and `ciris.ai/constitutional-mesh` (p. 7, p. 19).
- Leftover legacy terminology in `G24` and `G25`: "CC 7.1 Book IV ch. 4" and "CC 7.1 Book IV ch. 5" (p. 33) use a "Book" hierarchy alongside "Parts".

## Opportunities
- Deduplicating "0. The rule this piece is built on" and "0b. The eleven promises" (pp. 24–26)—which repeat pp. 2–10 almost word-for-word—down to a half-page pointer to the earlier sections would cut 2.5 pages and make the volume feel edited as a single book rather than four stitched files.
- Stripping commit hashes (`ca6bdf60`, `770156ef`, `ef02367`), PR numbers (`PR #1260`), and release-candidate markers (`rc8`, `re-pin 16`) from `G1`–`G28` (pp. 26–35) and leaving them in The Evidence Register volume would align this volume with house rules while keeping the table clean for readers.
- Breaking the 385-word semicolon chain on p. 7 into a bulleted list would make "The rules, plainly" actually plain.

## Threats
- **Internal contradiction on who may force an emergency stop:** Page 15 (`5g`) and page 21 ("Identity and upgrades") state that "only a root or an authority role can force a stop, and only for a safety emergency." However, page 4 (`5`), page 9 ("Two things no finding moves"), page 14 (`5a`), and page 24 state that "Any person may turn off, remove, or eject an agent from their own device, their own life, or a community they moderate, at any time, for any reason, without asking it." Furthermore, `G16` (p. 31) says "Only a ROOT or AUTHORITY principal may force a shutdown over the agent's objection" with no requirement that it be a "safety emergency," whereas `5b` (p. 14) says a shutdown over objection is done by "the steward, under the steward's own name." An implementer or lawyer cannot tell whether a steward/owner forcing a stop after a refused upgrade requires an emergency, a `ROOT`/`AUTHORITY` role, or simply the steward's signature after a 300-second window.
- **Internal contradiction on whether a guarantee row requires a witness or basis:** Page 25 ("Reviewer standing") states: "A reviewer of this piece may kill a row on either of two grounds: the row is not backed (no witness and no basis, and no ticket that will produce one)..." Yet `G14` (p. 30) has `Witness: —`, `Basis: —`, and no ticket, and `G25` and `G26` (pp. 33–34) have `Witness: none`, `Status: normative-only`, and no ticket. By its own rule on p. 25, a hostile reviewer must kill rows `G14`, `G25`, and `G26`.
- **Self-admitted governance defect on the Wise Authority board:** Pages 5 and 19 (`C2`) openly state that "The self-perpetuation of the Wise Authority board is a declared privilege with both cells missing: no condition ends it and the deferring party cannot decline it; so it is carried as a defect, and its remedy is pending." A regulator or hostile reviewer reading that agents must defer high-stakes decisions to a self-perpetuating board with no exit and no termination condition will treat this as an unresolved governance capture risk.
