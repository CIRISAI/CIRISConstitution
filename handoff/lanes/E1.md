# Lane E1: the one seat rule (analysis and a draft, no edits)

Read COMMON.md first.

Approved direction (the steward's, 2026-11-10): one seat rule in the text in place of nine
per-family gates. A reserved family names one duty; a row is admitted when its signer holds a
live delegation for that duty from whoever may confer it. This retires gates keyed on a
self-declared identity type and gives the roughly thirty reserved-but-ungated families a rule.
The model of the shape is the Wise Authority seat of CC 4.3 (a `delegates_to` with scope
`resolve_deferral`, conferred and withdrawn by the party it serves, enforced at admission,
judged at the row's signed instant inside the freshness window). The governing principles:
no authority rosters (anyone may assert under their own key; admission is trust or consent);
a seat is withdrawn by whoever conferred it; no node appoints anyone; every reserved role is
conferred by delegation and is enterable under any root (CC 3.2 T2, CC 4.4.3.8), the accord's
holders alone being fixed by the accord's own charter (CC 3.4.1).

Do this:
1. Inventory every reserved family: from `manifests/namespace_registry.json` (the `reserved`
   members and emitter rules), CC 3.4 and its subsections (3.4.1 to 3.4.14, especially 3.4.7,
   3.4.7.1, 3.4.7.2, 3.4.7.3), CC 4.4.3.8 item 6, CC 4.5.5, CC 4.3, the `signer_rule` column of
   `family_model.json`, `formal/derivation/signer_rule_text.tsv`, and the pinned open cells in
   `tools/check_transition_roles.py`. For each: today's gate in the text (quote it), today's
   cell, what the shipped store enforces if the cell says (`code-v61`), and whether the gate
   is keyed on an `identity_type` the key declares for itself.
2. Name the nine (or however many you find) per-family gates the one rule would replace, and
   the reserved families that have no gate at all.
3. For each reserved family, say what the one rule would read: the duty token (reuse an
   existing `delegates_to` scope token where one exists: `moderate`, `takedown`, `review`,
   `slash`, `resolve_deferral`, `infra:*`, `license`, `grant`, …; say plainly where none
   exists), who may confer it (a root the reader trusts, the party the duty serves, the
   community's authority set, the accord's charter), and whether admit/refuse would flip for
   any row that is admitted or refused today. Flips are the important column.
4. List what does NOT fit: families where the signer is fixed by something other than a
   conferred duty (a row only its subject may sign; a row only the store emits about itself;
   the accord holders). The one rule must not swallow these: say what rule they keep.
5. Draft the rule as it would stand in CC 3.4.7 (the entry: one table of who may sign a
   reserved family, then short paragraphs each stating one thing, sentences under 40 words,
   refusal tokens kept), and the sentence each replaced gate would be cut to.
6. List the decisions that are the steward's (anything that changes who may sign something
   people rely on) separately from what follows from the principles above.

Output: one markdown file at the path your launch message gives, with sections 1 to 6 as
above, tables where a table fits. Final message: the number of gates replaced, the number of
families that gain a rule, the number of admit/refuse flips, and the steward's decisions. Under 200 words.
