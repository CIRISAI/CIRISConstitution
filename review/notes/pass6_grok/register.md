## Weaknesses
- “Established” is defined as a resolvable symbol (p. 1) and then contradicted by the reading key: an impl/test/lean/bench tag “never certifies that the sentence it hangs on is true of the world” (p. 18). The generated register, which an auditor is told to read first (p. 1), has no strength column (p. 19) and still says each artifact “establishes” the claim (p. 59).
- Present tense is used for a staged rule. R14: “CC 4.4.2 aggregates signed dimensions by the mean of score × confidence” (p. 7). The matching row is staged (p. 106).
- A normative-only row states an empirical fact: agents “under minimal oversight are already deployed at consumer scale (Muse Glimmer, Dots, EO 14434)” (p. 61). The sources are unverifiable from here, and the page that supplies them already says the phrase “minimal oversight” is a reporter’s wording and “unverified” as the vendor’s (pp. 19–20).
- §8.4 is boxed as informative and as not touching the normative interior (p. 12), then §8.4.2.2 says the node “MUST emit” C2PA for generated media (p. 15).
- Two harm regimes do not match. CC 3.4.13: reporting suspected harm to a child is “a federation duty, not deferred to jurisdiction; silence is a slash-reachable breach” (p. 99). CC 7.3.5: a claim of harm “exists only where the law recognises one (CIRIS creates none)” and the federation “orders no remedy” (p. 125).
- Exit from stewardship is “only by the first-contact act” (p. 108) while “no such act is defined yet” (p. 61). Elsewhere “first contact” is device pairing (pp. 86–87).
- R5 is “Discharged” (p. 7) and “No longer deferred” (p. 10), but the register row is still titled “Hardware-class self-assertion gap (acknowledged)” and marked established (p. 104).
- “open 0” (pp. 57, 59) sits beside residual bets the text itself calls unverifiable, including the R9 side-information limb “not yet a register row” (p. 7) and the same 50%–5.8× floor and “45%” lying-model result repeated at p. 32.
- House style breaks throughout Part 8: history and workshop attribution (“trio Phase 4”, p. 11; “was Art 61 in the 2021 proposal”, pp. 23, 48, 52); editorializing (“a format nobody has implemented is not made real by ratifying a paragraph”, p. 11); first person (“We never adopt anyone’s semantics”, p. 12); comparative claims (the Web, p. 2; email authentication, p. 7; “C2PA has no consent architecture”, p. 14; SCITT “delta”, p. 16; Meta/OpenAI, pp. 19–20).

- Banned word in printed symbols: `test_drain_requests_graceful_shutdown_only` (p. 62) and `test_negotiation_timeout_forces_graceful_shutdown` (p. 126). Prose on p. 62 says “orderly”.
- Art. 50’s status cell is “Evidence-bearing” while the same cell says “legal sufficiency unreviewed” (p. 23). Annex C’s graduation rule has no slot for “Partial” or “Evidence-bearing” (p. 23).
- §8.6.4 names no sibling missions (p. 20). Annex letters are stored out of order: 8.8.2 Annex B, 8.8.3 Annex D, 8.8.5 Annex C (p. 21). Annex J restarts subsection numbers (1.1 under §7, 2.1 under §8, 3.1 under §9, 5.1 under §11; pp. 55–57).

## Threats
- A regulator or platform lawyer can quote p. 61 as a finding of fact about named products and Executive Order 14434. Those citations are unverifiable from here; one false title or date collapses the factual premise of the stewardship duties.
- A lawyer can quote p. 99 (federation duty, silence is slash-reachable, “not deferred to jurisdiction”) against p. 125 (no CIRIS cause of action, jurisdictional process, no remedy) and against Annex I’s “legal sufficiency is unreviewed” (p. 43).
- An implementer can treat 370 “established” rows as proof of the prose. The volume’s own grade key says that reading is not licensed (p. 18), and the register they are told to trust does not carry the grade (p. 19).
- “open 0” (p. 59) and “The moderation surface is complete” (p. 8) will be quoted against R9–R14, the deferred workshops (pp. 11–12), and 106 staged rows.
- Annex G §8.2 gives a religious text a veto-shaped role in an engineering change (p. 36) while §8.6.1 says informational citations have “no normative force” (p. 17). A secular regulator will treat that as undeclared authority.
- “First-contact” as both the undefined exit from stewardship (pp. 61, 108) and the device-join ceremony (pp. 86–87) will be read as an operational path that does not exist.
- Annex thresholds (10^26-or-1026 FLOP, $10M/day, RS ≥ 0.97, 200 ms abort, 9-member WA board; pp. 21–22, 26, 28) bind adopters (p. 21) and are outside the register by declaration. A hostile reviewer will not accept the carve-out.
