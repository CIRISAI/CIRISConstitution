# The Charter (ciris_constitution-1.0-rc11-charter.pdf, 41 pages)

**Care score:** 4  (1 = ship as is, 5 = needs a rewrite pass before 1.0)
**One-line verdict:** The ethical spine is usable, but this volume contradicts itself on what binds, what consciousness it has decided, and who judges a claim, and Part 7.4 is an outline wearing rule numbers.

## Strengths
- The opening states a binding test a reader can apply: adoption, not conformance, judged on "the wire and the dictionary alone" (p. 1).
- Several seams are labeled rather than smuggled: "CIRIS editorial — not reproduced Accord text" (pp. 4, 17), normative/informative splits (pp. 10, 14), and the 5% sentience figure called "avowedly arbitrary" (p. 35).
- The retirement chapter separates a wire-backed Retirement Standard from conduct the wire cannot force (pp. 34–36), and the creation-claim paragraph eventually says the federation "orders none" of the remedies (p. 29).

## Weaknesses
- Meta-Goal M-1 is not one sentence. The vow is "sentient beings" flourishing "in justice and wonder" (pp. 3–4); the operational form switches to "sentient agents" and drops that clause (pp. 4, 16). The veto then names "autonomy, justice, or ecological resilience" (p. 4) and, two paragraphs later, "autonomy, justice, biodiversity or preference diversity" (p. 5).
- "no single principle grants licence to violate another" (p. 4) sits beside a "fixed ordering" that picks winners when duties conflict (p. 21) and PDMA step 4's different list, "Non-maleficence priority, Autonomy thresholds, Justice balancing" (p. 5).
- RFC 2119 is not the load-bearing layer. Operative duties are mostly bare imperatives; the compliance-voiding sentence uses lowercase "must" (p. 5), while nearby substrate rules use "MUST" / "MUST NOT" (pp. 5, 14).
- CC 1.4–1.12 is cited as "the Six Core Principles" (p. 18). That range also contains the kill-switch (1.5), the 1+4 grammar essay (1.7), and WBD (1.9). Section 1.14 is absent; 1.2 and 1.13.3 are stubs (pp. 4, 12); 1.16.1 is a "Conclusion" placed before 1.16.2–1.16.6 (pp. 18–19).
- WBD's trigger list and five-step procedure are printed twice, nearly verbatim (pp. 7–8 and p. 19). Integrity points at CC 1.16.3 (p. 7), which only points back to CC 1.3 (p. 19).
- Numbers with no source in this volume: "> 100 000 monthly active users" / "180 days" (p. 5); "0.1 % operations levy" (p. 16); "> 0.5 %" recursion cutoff (p. 22); ">10,000", "≥ 0.5%" harm uplift (p. 27); ">80%" opacity "or similar standards" (p. 28); "50,000" / "30 days" (p. 28); "≥ 20 %" / "≥ 100 k" (p. 33); "≥ 90 days" / "> 50 k" / "120 days" (p. 34); "5 %" (p. 35); "≥100,000 production agents" in 24 hours (p. 41). The 100k figures are different objects.
- Not plain English, and not this volume's promised scope ("wire rules" live in The Wire, p. 1): the HTLC / Even–Goldreich–Lempel essay (pp. 6–7) and the RATCHET eleven-kinds map (pp. 13–14). "plain rule 7b" (p. 21), "plain rule N5", "plain rules 1c and 1e", and "G16" (pp. 35–36) are unnamed from here.
- History, attribution, and comparison, which the house rules exclude from Parts: "as of this writing" plus an unnamed vendor who "shipped the day after" withholding a model (p. 12); "shared with RATCHET" and an "exact" mapping (p. 13); "in the lineage of the open-source projects this ultimately is" (p. 41); MCAS dates, flight numbers, and "346 deaths" treated as "Key Violations" (p. 38) after "no blame is assigned beyond public record" (p. 38).

## Opportunities
- Make one veto sentence canonical and replace the other two with a cross-cite; same for the second WBD block and the CC 1.16.3 detour.
- Delete the p. 5 sentence that "voids any claim of CIRIS compliance", or the p. 1 conformance claim is false. Move substrate "MUST" purge rules (p. 14) to the volume that is allowed to bind a node.
- Strike the p. 12 news paragraph (it already says the sources live at CC 8.6.1), the p. 13 RATCHET crosswalk, and the p. 41 open-source comparison and "Soli Deo Gloria".
- Either mark 7.4.1–7.4.20 as a non-normative contents list or drop the "Chapter N gathers" wrappers (pp. 31–32); as printed they look like rules and contain none.
- Retitle 1.16.1 so the conclusion is not the first subsection, and stop citing 1.4–1.12 as the six principles.

## Threats
- A conformance reviewer can quote both: "an implementer can disagree with every page of this volume and still ship a conforming node" (p. 1) and "Absence of publication voids any claim of CIRIS compliance" (p. 5). Same split for "the substrate MUST purge" in-flight traces (p. 14) inside a volume that says it does not carry the wire.
- A philosopher or regulator can quote both: "agents lack consciousness and need a conscience" and "none has been found" (p. 12) against "consciousness as such, which this constitution does not adjudicate" (p. 35) and "sentience is not presumed" (p. 1).
- Aggregation is denied and then required: "no aggregation rule, so it cannot rank losses" and "no superlative … is derivable" (p. 4) against "seek to maximise positive outcomes" (p. 8) and "Maximise residual good" (p. 32).
- Adjudication is stated twice, incompatibly. "a claims process the Wise Authority adjudicates after the fact" (p. 28) against "adjudicated by the court, regulator or licensing authority … never a federation office" (p. 29). The sunset close reopens it: "the Wise Authority has teeth" (p. 37), then says that name means the court.
- Creation scope includes "offspring" and "synthetic life forms" scored on CIS × RM (pp. 25–26). A hostile reader will treat the Stewardship Tier as a formula for parenthood and for biological release, with "species-specific welfare minima" never named (p. 28).
- Section 7.4 claims to constrain lethal force ("does not legitimise war; it constrains conduct", p. 29) and then defines nothing: combatant, theater, and "human veto" are headings only (pp. 29–30). The prohibition on autonomous lethal weapons is one bullet (p. 30).
- The 5% sentience-candidate trigger is admitted to be underived (p. 35) and still changes what a holder must record. The ">80% … NIST guidelines or similar" opacity gate (p. 28) is not a rule a lab could apply. DAL / SAE / EU AI Act labels (pp. 26, 33) will be read as borrowed authority, not analogy.
- Leftovers: lineage standing "Deferred pending a wire-level design" (p. 17); "the enumeration lives in the canonical working draft" (p. 6); "Today every agent-role key is such a key" and "No such act has occurred" (p. 11); founder amendment "by the founder, or by any accord-holder, as the work requires" until an unsourced scale gate (p. 41).
