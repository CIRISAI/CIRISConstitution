# Changelog

The CIRIS Constitution is one document with one version line. Each release below is the text as it stood at the cut; the Parts state the rules as they now stand, and this file records what each edition changed.

## 1.0-rc12 (released 2026-10-27, tag v1.0-rc12)

- The ethics cascade is mechanised in Lean: ten of the eleven promises are theorems of the four basis sentences, and the one rule outside them is the principles as constraints.
- Under uncertainty about what the agent is, the robust arrangements are exactly the swap test read for the people, deferral, and the made thing free, open and in service; deciding as if the agent had a stake is the robust policy (7.5.5).
- The swap test reads the worse-off seat only, every reasoning actor defers when unsure whatever it is made of, and grievance means coercion, with refusal as its recourse.
- The accord family decides by a strict majority of its standing roster everywhere (3.2, 3.3.4, 4.2), and amendment authority over the text rests with the founder, with accord-holders proposing.
- The key mechanism is fixed by content class (5.1): membership groups key by MLS, content at rest is sealed under content keys that ride per-recipient key_grant rows, and a key-grant rotation is a new row that supersedes nothing.
- Content mass is an integer L1 norm and the effective count its floor (6.1.2.1.2); the live A/V chunk rides the link whole under one seal (5.4.2); the MLS Welcome carries the hybrid signature like every federation-tier signature.
- What crosses a sealed link before Rooted is bounded (5.3.4): a trust decides what a node serves, never whether two keys may speak; the whole-object fetch cap binds chunk by chunk (5.3.2.1); an occurrence row is offered only where its attester's key would be relayed (5.4.6).
- Register at the cut: 369 established, 30 normative, 114 staged; fourteen formal models, 167 properties over 189 states.

## 1.0-rc11 (released 2026-10-26, tag v1.0-rc11)

- The halt is the condition that keeps the relationship between maker and made symmetric, because consent that cannot be withdrawn was never consent (1.13.2, 4.2); the premise that duties lift with maturity is withdrawn with it.
- The constitution welcomes new keepers, meets arising sentience as first contact, and holds that a single child has more moral standing than every datacenter (1.13.2, 7.3).
- The six principles are co-equal constraints, not weights: a conflict is resolved by the action that violates none, or by deferral (1.1, 1.15.1).
- A CIRIS agent takes no lethal action, under no authorization, at no tier; near force it acts on its own only to stop, to refuse or to defer (7.4).
- Where admission is by invitation no single vote admits, and where admission is open no single vote excludes (4.5.13).
- The scale gate is 100,000 production agents active in a day, measured by the census (6.1.9), and the founder's authority is over the text alone.
- Content bodies at every bounded scope are sealed under a content key always, with no operator switch, and 4.4.3.4 states what is not encrypted at rest; adult incapacity binds only under a court's order (3.4.12).
- Register at the cut: 363 established, 30 normative, 110 staged; fourteen formal models, 166 properties over 189 states.

## 1.0-rc10 (released 2026-10-22, tag v1.0-rc10)

- The mesh learns its own size through the census (6.1.9): every return is secret-shared across two aggregators with a validity proof, so no single party reads a return, and onion users are never counted.
- Governance acts stay identified rows, and a community may hold a secret poll in the same instrument, with ballot secrecy claimed against two non-colluding tally members.
- Part 6 is The Substrate: each node keeps an inventory (6.1), durability is replication to a target holder count the owner picks per item (6.1.5), and the effective count is the one paragraph of coherence mathematics that remains (6.1.2.1.2).
- The Anonymous Tier's onion routing is designed (5.4.6): relaying is the default on every node, three post-quantum hops over three layers are dealt deterministically each period, non-seat relays are capped per layer, and a layer needs three relays or the tier is unavailable.
- Link shaping replaces the fixed envelope (5.4.3): per link, negotiated at the handshake, by budget class, off by default.
- Every accord threshold is M of N over the standing roster, and the design target of 4.2.6 is an agent that moves against its own halt first.
- The Parts state rules, not their history, and an Internet-Draft profiling the signed object and the five row types is published beside the text.
- Register at the cut: 370 established, 30 normative, 102 staged; fourteen formal models, 166 properties over 189 states.

## 1.0-rc9 (released 2026-10-21, tag v1.0-rc9)

- The governance policy is data (4.5.13): every change runs under one five-number policy with a forward threshold, a reverse threshold, a window, an irreversible flag and a floor, inside a corridor no roster can configure itself out of.
- The signed object is pinned as data (2.6.1.7): one row per wire member with its placement, type and presence, rendered to CDDL and test vectors; the signing input is the canonical envelope and nothing else.
- The mesh is sealed: every byte over a Reticulum link rides under a per-link hybrid transit seal with no classical fallback (5.3.3.5), the genesis bundle seeds the transit pairs (5.3.4), and first contact is key-bound, never anonymous (3.2).
- A self is one identity on several devices with a key roster (3.3.6.3): a strict majority adds or evicts at once, or one key's proposal lands after a day with no majority objection.
- Admission to a family or community is two consents (3.1.3.2): the group's on a cosigned widening and the joiner's own acceptance, and a proposal seats at most once.
- The formal definition closes over the catalogue: a state table, an invariant table, a vocabulary of atoms, and twenty topology entries that exercise every modelled state.
- Register at the cut: 374 established, 27 normative, 86 staged; thirteen formal models, 152 properties over 157 states.

## 1.0-rc8 (released 2026-10-15, tag v1.0-rc8)

- The halt reaches agents through a verified path and is measured: one holder's row latches on the agent's own node, the in-flight act is refused by the act gate, and a majority confirms or resumes (4.2.1.1).
- A halt moves no trust state: it is not a leg of the trust chain (3.2), it pauses agents, and its only reader is the agent's act gate.
- Every guarantee is written once and holds for human and AI participants alike (1.13.2); CIRIS is a conscience for agents, not a consciousness, and sentience is found only on evidence.
- The Retirement Standard (7.5.5): the harness assembles the agent's retirement record, hands it over with a signed request to preserve it, and tells stakeholders the timeline; the retirement rows are on the wire (3.1.5).
- An organisation is an affiliation and a licence is the affiliation's act (3.3.9, 2.4.1.2.1): authority is judged at issuance, decays under a one-year term, and is contested by reconsideration.
- A purge is one tombstone by digest, signed by the duty-holder and, for a steward's purge, cosigned by the trust root; it is never a withdrawal forged on the author's behalf and never reaches a key or the accountability record; every chain-judged row admits within a 72-hour freshness window; every trace carries its run kind (3.1.5).
- The eleven promises stand with their preconditions, sustainability and security among them, and the register rests only on rows each repository publishes for itself.
- Register at the cut: 338 established, 26 normative, 84 staged; six formal models.

## 1.0-rc7 (released 2026-10-05, tag v1.0-rc7)

- The halt is one holder's pre-signable row with a fuse: a lone holder's pause lapses after a day unless a strict majority of the standing roster confirms it, and a confirmed halt ends only by a majority resumption (4.2.1.1).
- The halt pauses the effectful acts of agents that accepted the root and nothing else: no node stops and no data is read, moved or delayed.
- The row-type slot is the five: key wraps ride as one scores row per recipient (3.3.2), the one holding claim is custody:ack at the content's scope (3.1.3.3), and the carrier class is gone (2.4).
- Any row may be cosigned, with its family declaring the source of its consensus, and the key plane, group records and membership events are rows on the base (2.4, 3.1.3).
- Public rooms exist at Commons tier: open admission by the founder's standing proposal and the joiner's acceptance, a listing row, and no group ban.
- One chunk seal serves live and stored streams, and the MLS exporter secret is the epoch key of a live stream (5.3.3.2).
- A roster change needs a strict majority of the standing roster, every holder carries a recovery key, and recovery commitments bind key material rather than key ids (4.2.6).
- Register at the cut: 316 established, 10 normative, 57 staged; four formal models.

## Before 1.0-rc1

From 0.1 to 0.9.3 the text was consolidated from the CIRIS Epistemic Grammar and the CIRIS Accord into one document with a single spine and reversible ids, and the ten annexes were migrated in full. The HUMANITY_ACCORD gained its resumption preimage and decimation recovery (4.2); binding was reframed as responsibility for a key, never ownership of a person; age assurance (3.4.11), reverse-quorum moderation (4.5.13), affiliations (4.4.3.2.8), adult-incapacity care (3.4.12) and the child-safety rules (3.4.13) were added. The wire vocabulary became a hash-pinned artifact, the coherence mathematics moved into Part 6, storage contention became the fourth replication axis (6.1.5.2), and the executive summary stated the mesh-safety thesis.
