# The Mathematics (ciris_constitution-1.0-rc11-maths.pdf, 21 pages)

**Care score:** 3
**One-line verdict:** Substantively rigorous and candid about its security bounds, but undermined by a front-matter contradiction with the body, legacy Evolution/v1–v3 changelog residue, code-path pointers, and a volume lens ("The Mathematics") that does not match its wire-protocol contents.

## Strengths
- Explicit and honest scoping of the privacy boundary against a pinned baseline adversary $A_0$, expressly prohibiting implementations from claiming erasure or anonymity against side-informed adversaries (pp. 4–5).
- Clear disambiguation between the two distinct "Kish" formulas (`n_eff` mass-weighted participation ratio vs. `k_eff` equicorrelated source discount) and the safe vs. exposed thresholding directions of `k_eff` (pp. 7–9).
- Frank statement of the enforcement limits of prohibited-content purges over a pull-only replication plane (pp. 9–10) and of the lack of coercion resistance and seat-assignment enforcement in the census (p. 21).
- Clean, byte-exact binary preimage specifications for `AggregationMetaV1`, `StorageBudgetV1`, and `CorpusWantV1` with explicit post-quantum signature binding rules (pp. 6, 15).

## Weaknesses
- **Front-matter contradictions on mechanisation:** Page 1 states inside two paragraphs both that `k_eff` is "(the one result here with a Lean witness)" and that "One witness is mechanised, the census ballot model under `formal/census_ballot`". In the body, both are mechanised: `k_eff` at `lean:coherence-ratchet:Core.BaseIdentity` (p. 9) and the census at `formal/census_ballot` (p. 21).
- **Front-matter contradiction on scope vs. normative body:** Page 1 claims "Whom it binds. Nobody directly... What it leaves out. The engineering: codec choices, chunk sizes and endpoint shapes are in The Wire." Yet Part 6 is titled "The Substrate" (p. 2), is overwhelmingly marked `(normative)`, contains ~80 RFC 2119 `MUST` / `MUST NOT` mandates, and specifies binary wire layouts, domain separators, and storage budgets (`AggregationMetaV1` p. 6, `StorageBudgetV1` and `CorpusWantV1` p. 15).
- **Code/repo pointers in normative prose:** "under `formal/census_ballot`" (pp. 1, 21), "`aggregation_meta/canonical_bytes_v3`" (p. 6), and "`lean:coherence-ratchet:Core.BaseIdentity` ... and `Core.SignalSourceDiscount.clique_neutralization`" (p. 9) violate the house rule that the register pins repositories and paths, not the prose.
- **Version history and design-iteration residue:** Section 6.1.2.1 (pp. 6–8) reads as an evolutionary changelog from v1 to v2 to v3 ("unchanged at v3", "v2+: signed effective source count", "v3: largest content-similarity cluster size", "The v2 and v3 signed members close those two blindnesses in that order", "since v3 a lying n_eff is checkable", "which is why a mass-only surface was insufficient and why v3 exists") even though p. 6 mandates that `version = 3` is the only valid version with no deprecation window.
- **Unanchored acronyms:** `CEG` (p. 2, "enter CEG as additive normative sections", and throughout), `CEWP-1.0` (p. 12, "A CEWP-1.0 conformant node"), `ALM` (p. 11, expanded only on p. 18), `STH` (p. 2), and `SFU` (p. 18) are used without local definition.
- **Undefined constant in normative rule:** Page 21 states a floor rule for "the standing roster at or below the small-community size, and above it the larger of the roster’s rooted-active members and half of the last stable count", never defining what number "the small-community size" is or which section pins it.
- **Tautological sentence from editing:** "The version members are appended in version order — u32_be(n_eff) at v2, then u32_be(max_source_multiplicity) ∥ mass_commitment[32] at v3 — so the fields are appended in version order;" (p. 6).

## Opportunities
- Reconcile page 1 ("What this is", "Whom it binds", "What it promises", "What it leaves out") with the actual contents of Part 6: acknowledge that both the effective-count ratchet and the census ballot model are mechanised, and state accurately that this volume prints the normative rules of Part 6 (The Substrate) alongside their mathematical rationales.
- Strip the v1/v2 evolutionary narrative from 6.1.2.1 and 6.1.2.1.2 (pp. 6–8): state the `version = 3` preimage and its gates as timeless 1.0 rules, keeping only the one-sentence wire note that `b"AGG-META-v1\0\0\0\0\0"` is retained as the literal 16-byte domain separator alongside `version = 3`.
- Move literal file/module paths (`formal/census_ballot`, `lean:coherence-ratchet:...`, `aggregation_meta/canonical_bytes_v3`) to the register and cite them by register row ID, matching the promise on p. 1.

## Threats
- **Implementer/lawyer challenge on binding status:** Because page 1 explicitly declares "Whom it binds. Nobody directly", an implementer can argue that the 80+ `MUST`s in Part 6 (including PQC signature verification on `StorageBudgetV1`, the `R9` multiplicity gate, and the prohibited-content carve-out in 6.1.2.2) are non-binding commentary unless duplicated in *The Wire*.
- **Unresolved wire vs. attestation conflict in 6.1.9:** Section 6.1.3 (p. 10) states categorically: "Every CC 6.1 object is transport/substrate framing — it never instantiates a CC 2.1 Contribution, never adds an attestation_type, never enters CC 2.6.1 JCS canonicalization." Yet Section 6.1.9 (p. 20) states: "The composite is a CC 2.1 `scores` row whose signed members are the inputs of CC 6.1.2.1 — `version = 3`, `source_count`, `n_eff`, `max_source_multiplicity`, `mass_commitment_hex`, `member_commitment_hex`, `aggregation_algorithm_id` — verified at federation admission by the dominance gate". Furthermore, `max_source_multiplicity` is a mandatory signed member on that census composite row even though the previous paragraph (p. 20) says "The multiplicity gate of CC 6.1.2.1 is a corpus-fold gate and does not read census returns or ballots", leaving the required value of `max_source_multiplicity` on a census composite unspecified.
- **Ambiguous graph-construction rule in the R9 multiplicity gate (p. 8):** Section 6.1.2.1.2 pins `similarity metric` (1 - normalized L1 distance), `threshold` (0.950), and `clustering` ("the largest connected component of the similarity graph"), and calls this "a fork in admissibility, with valid signatures on both sides" if two implementations diverge. However, it never explicitly states the edge predicate of the similarity graph (`similarity(i, j) >= 0.950` vs `> 0.950`) or the exact fixed-point scaling denominator for "1 - normalized L1 distance" before comparing to integer `950` in milli-units.
