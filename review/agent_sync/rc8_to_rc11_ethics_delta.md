# The ethics text and the guides: rc8 → rc11 delta for CIRISAgent

For CIRISAgent's `constitution_ethics_1.3_*` texts (29 locales and the polyglot edition), the comprehensive guides, and the accord pins, moving from "an instantiation of the CIRIS Constitution 1.0-rc8 (CIRISAI/CIRISConstitution @ b833c43, 2026-10-13)" to 1.0-rc11. Everything below is the CC side: the passages the ethics text instantiates, stated as old → new in English against the CC source at the rc11 release. The agent's localization sources (`localization/accord/constitution_ethics_1.3/{lang}.json`) are theirs to regenerate from these.

**Header.** rc11 is the merge commit `9a5a249` on `main` (PR #187, 2026-10-26); the pdf-finalize commit that carries the annotated tag `v1.0-rc11` follows it on `main` and is the commit to cite in the header once tagged: "an instantiation of the CIRIS Constitution 1.0-rc11 (CIRISAI/CIRISConstitution @ <finalize sha>, 2026-10-26)". The release note is the CHANGELOG's 1.0-rc11 section.

**Translations.** None are provided here and none have been audited: this document is the English delta only. The agent's sub-agent translation of the changed passages, followed by its native-language audit before shipping, is the pipeline; every locale is structural-only until that audit. The polyglot edition's braided passages that restate these sections should be regenerated from the English after it.

**Book IX, first, because it is the one structural change.** Book IX "states the mathematics of coherence as the CIRIS Constitution states it (1.0-rc4, Part 6 … CC 6.2.1, 6.2.5)". Section 6.2 was removed at rc10 and Part 6 is now *The Substrate* (6.1: canonicalisation boundary, the census, replication, the wholeness witness, the noise floor, the effective source count). The collapse geometry, the convex-cut theorem and the "capacity analysis under chosen constraints" are no longer stated anywhere in the Constitution; the one piece kept is the Kish effective count at 6.1.2.1.2. Book IX therefore no longer instantiates a CC section. Two honest options, the steward's to pick: retire Book IX from the ethics text, or keep it labelled as the agent's own explanatory text with no CC citation (not "as the CIRIS Constitution states it"). The header line that says "Book IX / Section IX follows CC Part 6 … (CC 6.2.1, 6.2.5)" goes either way.

> **What this document does not govern.** A system built to take lethal action is built under some other framework; this document does not govern it and lends it no key and no name. A deployment that gives an agent a lethal capability has not adopted this charter, whatever its wire conformance says: conformance is judged on the wire ([CC 1.13.3]), and this is a duty of adoption.
