# The Mathematics

**What this is.** Part 6: the canonicalisation boundary and the 1+4 line, the substrate — the census, replication, the wholeness witness — with its noise-floor model, the effective source count that gates aggregation (the one result here with a Lean witness), and the census with its model.

**Whom it binds.** Nobody directly. A theorem binds by being true. What binds implementers is stated in *The Wire*; what this volume adds is the argument that the wire's guarantees hold under the stated assumptions, and the named places where the argument is weaker than its statement.

**What it promises.** The substrate's rules with the arguments behind them: the noise floor as a checkable predicate, the effective count stated with its clamp, its ceiling and its exposed direction, the descent and replication rules, and the census. One witness is mechanised, the census ballot model under `formal/census_ballot`, and the volume names it where it bears; where an argument stands without a model, the text says so.

**What it leaves out.** The engineering: codec choices, chunk sizes and endpoint shapes are in *The Wire*. The ethics the mathematics serves are in *The Charter*.

**Where the rest lives.** The effective count's Lean witness and the census model are named in the register, which pins the repositories they live in. A reviewer who wants to kill a result should start from the register row, not from the prose.
