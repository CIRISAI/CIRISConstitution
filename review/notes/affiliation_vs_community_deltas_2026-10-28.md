# Community vs affiliation: the formal deltas, cited

Read against branch `rc13` (text = 1.0-rc12). Line numbers are `constitution/<file>:<line>`; P2 = `part_2_the_grammar.md`, P3 = `part_3_the_namespace.md`, P4 = `part_4_composition_governance.md`, P5 = `part_5_transport_substrate.md`, P6 = `part_6_the_coherence_mathematics.md`. Each row is marked **TEXT SAYS** (with the sentence) or **SILENT, needs a ruling**. Where two sections disagree the row says so.

**The one sentence that frames everything.** An affiliation is not a record kind. P3:1592 (CC 3.3.9): "The Portal's `Organization` is a community at `cohort_scope: affiliations` ([CC 4.4.3.2.8]) and nothing about an organisation is a record kind of its own." P4:645 (CC 4.4.3.2.8): "The discriminator is the single `cohort_scope` value; affiliations **share the CommunityDek crypto tier** and all the community machinery (DEK cascade, forward secrecy on removal, `consensus_protocol` admission), and add **institutional governance by construction** through one declared config record." So every delta below is either (a) something the config record adds, or (b) something the text never said about the `community` record once its content sits at `affiliations`.


## The steward's three reasons, checked against the text

The steward (2026-10-28): "affiliations are afforded more leeway in their moderation/dealings than communities, as they lack the pro-social assumption of communities — there are also legal considerations applied to businesses around data handling/retention we put on the affiliations tier and exclude from community handling." Each table row below carries a **Why** cell keyed to these findings.

**(a) The pro-social assumption of a community.** The words "pro-social" do not occur. What the text carries:
- **A1 — TEXT SAYS the split, as motive.** CC 4.4.3.2.8, P4:645: "A **community** gathers by *interest* — voluntary, opt-in, no role compels it. An **affiliation** gathers by *necessity* — the work, institution, or office a person belongs to because their role requires it." P4:647: "A group that needs **no** institutional governance is simply a `community`." The split is stated, but it motivates *more* machinery for affiliations ("institutional governance by construction"), never less.
- **A2 — TEXT SAYS the floors, for "a community", with no affiliation exemption.** Admission and removal: CC 3.1.3.2, P3:215 "two consents, never one"; CC 4.5.13, P4:1759 "never fewer than two voices in a community ... `founder_only` ... a community above the family that names it is refused"; `governance_policy.tsv` rows `community_invite` / `community_remove` (2/3 enact, 259 200 s). Moderation: CC 4.5.4, P4:1443 "A `community` is admitted, and continues to federate, **only while ≥1 member holds the live `moderate` duty**"; CC 4.5.13, P4:1733 "For a takedown / harm report, the window's **default outcome is removal** ... **Passive survival is impossible**." Because CC 3.3.9 (P3:1592) makes an affiliation "a community at `cohort_scope: affiliations`", every one of these floors reaches it as the text stands. Nothing says the floors rest on a pro-social assumption, and nothing lifts them where that assumption is absent.
- **A3 — TEXT SAYS the protective default, for both.** CC 4.4.3.2.1, P4:403: "This gives a persecuted community a cryptographic home"; CC 1.13.3.4, P1:248 smallest-scope default. The same DEK tier covers `affiliations` (P4:408), so this is no asymmetry.

**(b) Wider moderation latitude for affiliations.** **SILENT**, and the sentences that touch it go the other way:
- **B1 — the text tightens, not loosens.** CC 4.4.3.2.8, P4:670: `hierarchy` is "generalizing the CC 4.5.13 steward/moderator tiers" and "**Requires** term-bound authority: every role edge, and every edge carrying an issuance or moderation scope, carries `delegation_valid_until` ... A lapsed `moderate` edge is a moderator lapse under CC 4.5.4". CC 4.5.13, P4:1751: "Where the duty-holder acts for an organisation, the organisation is on the bond" — accountability added, not latitude. CC 3.2, P3:805: `slash` and `quarantine` "reach the affiliation's keys as well as the steward's." P3:1592: "A delegated holder never stands in for the `consensus_protocol` on a roster or config change."
- **B2 — the latitude the text does give is over the affiliation's own data and subjects, each with a declared legal basis, never over moderation floors.** `erasure_policy`: "a right-to-be-forgotten / N5 signal is **refused-with-reason** rather than honored (GDPR Art 17(3)(b) ...)" (P4:656); `legal_hold` "**overrides both** the retention schedule **and** consent-driven descent" (P4:655); `compartments[]` with "per-member EXCLUSIONS" (P4:661); `access_control_model: minimum-necessary` with "audited **break-the-glass**" (P4:663); `lawful_access` "DECLARED, scope-bounded **to the declaring affiliation only** ... **NEVER a covert backdoor**" (P4:675); `independent_branch` oversight bodies "exempt from the chain's read/control authority" (P4:670). None of these touches CC 4.5.4 or CC 4.5.13.

**(c) Legal data-handling and retention duties on the affiliations tier, excluded from communities.** **TEXT SAYS**, with one shared floor:
- **C1 — affiliation-only limbs.** CC 4.4.3.2.8, P4:651-657: `classification_scheme`, `retention_policy` (per-class "**floor** (`retain_minimum` / `permanent`)", "**ceiling** (`delete_after`)", `legal_basis`), `legal_hold`, `erasure_policy`, `disclosure_posture`; P4:665 `archive_custody`; P4:666 `chain_of_custody`; P4:671 `designated_officials` "(Privacy + Security Officer / Controller-DPO where the party is the controller per CC 8.8.9 Annex I; Records Officer; FOIA Officer ...)"; P4:676-679 `transparency_obligations[]`, `disclosure_accounting` (HIPAA 164.528), `breach_notification`, `processor_chain` / `baa`; P4:685 "`regulatory_profile` and `jurisdiction[]` ... load the relevant **Annex I §3.2 sector overlay** ... The SI ≥ 0.6 Controller rule selects the accountable authority." The registered `hard_case:legal_hold_set / _lifted / _violation_attempt` kinds cite only CC 7.5.5 and CC 4.4.3.2.8 (P3:521).
- **C2 — the community record carries none of them.** The `community {}` struct (P3:579-594) has no classification, retention, hold or official field; its one retention knob is `archive_mode`, which "is **operator-local config, NOT federated trust state** — it rides no attestation" (P3:680).
- **C3 — the subject-consent floors bind every producer in both tiers.** N5, P6:175: "retention MUST NOT keep alive, **above the noise floor**, content whose consent is withdrawn"; erasure at request, CC 4.5.3, P4:1435: an ordinary row by the subject's `withdraws`, an adverse row "by the row's **producer** under rule 1, the data controller withdrawing its own row, or refused with reason under Article 17(3) (CC 4.4 `erasure_policy`)" — the one community-side sentence that reaches into the affiliation limb; the deletion-SLA watcher, P4:1034; the CC 4.5.2.2 table is "informational" and "CEG does NOT prescribe which regulatory framework an operator MUST comply with" (P4:1381, 1397). "Controller" attaches by role, not by tier: Annex I, P8:1177 / 1195 "Controller duties attach to the party that determines the purposes and means of the processing." The takedown reporting duties attach to "an operator under US jurisdiction" (P4:1424), a role orthogonal to both kinds.
- **C4 — the words.** "business" occurs only in CC 3.3.9 ("everyone's private business detail stays home", P3:1596; "PII and business detail ... NEVER emitted", P3:1600); "employee" once (P3:1594, the steward not punished for refusing); "employer" and "operator of record" nowhere.

**Net.** Reason (c) is carried by the text as written and is affiliation-only. Reason (a) is carried as a motive (A1) but not as a relaxation: the floors it would relax bind both kinds (A2). Reason (b) is not in the text; where the text speaks it adds duties to affiliations. If the steward wants (a) and (b) to be true, rows 3c and 3g are where they have to be written, and the register's community floors would need an affiliation column.
