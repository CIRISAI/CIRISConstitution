# Changelog

The CIRIS Constitution is one document with one version line. Each release below is the text as it stood at the cut; the Parts state the rules as they now stand, and this file records what each edition changed.

## 1.0-rc11 (released 2026-10-26, tag v1.0-rc11)

## Before 1.0-rc1

From 0.1 to 0.9.3 the text was consolidated from the CIRIS Epistemic Grammar and the CIRIS Accord into one document with a single spine and reversible ids, and the ten annexes were migrated in full. The HUMANITY_ACCORD gained its resumption preimage and decimation recovery (4.2); binding was reframed as responsibility for a key, never ownership of a person; age assurance (3.4.11), reverse-quorum moderation (4.5.13), affiliations (4.4.3.2.8), adult-incapacity care (3.4.12) and the child-safety rules (3.4.13) were added. The wire vocabulary became a hash-pinned artifact, the coherence mathematics moved into Part 6, storage contention became the fourth replication axis (6.1.5.2), and the executive summary stated the mesh-safety thesis.
