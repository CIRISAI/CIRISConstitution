# The Evidence Register (ciris_constitution-1.0-rc11-register.pdf, 131 pages)

**Care score:** 3
**One-line verdict:** The register spine and epistemic-grading discipline are strong, but the volume needs a cleanup pass to fix two direct governance contradictions, strip leaked HTML/markdown build artifacts, and scrub residual marketing and banned words before 1.0.

## Strengths
- Epistemic honesty in CC 8.6.1 ("Strength grades — how to read an evidence row", pp. 18–19), which explicitly warns auditors not to confuse a machine-checked `lean:` or `impl:` pointer with empirical truth of the prose claim.
- Candid scoping of technical and legal limits, notably the private-content CSAM detection wall (CC 8.3.2, p. 9), the `R1`–`R14` wager table (CC 8.3.1, pp. 7–8), and the non-citable negative transfer signal in Annex J (CC 8.8.10 §2–3, p. 54).
- Clear separation of operational target annexes from normative conformance rules (CC 8.8 standing note, p. 21) and explicit boundary modes for external standards (CC 8.4.1, p. 13).
- Machine-checked traceability across 503 load-bearing claims (pp. 59–131) with explicit resolution against implementation symbols, conformance tests, formal models, or staging tickets.

## Weaknesses
- **Leaked HTML comments and raw markdown links:** Literal `<!– stale-ok –>` strings render in the PDF at the end of both paragraphs on p. 20 (CC 8.6.1), and a raw markdown link `[evidence_refs[]](part_2_the_grammar.md)` renders literally on p. 13 (CC 8.4.1).
- **Broken section and clause numbering:** Top-level headings skip `8.7` (jumping from `8.6.4` on p. 21 to `8.8` on p. 21) and skip `8.8.1` and `8.8.4` (jumping `8.8` → `8.8.2` → `8.8.3` → `8.8.5` on pp. 21–22). Inside Annex J (CC 8.8.10, pp. 55–57), sub-clause numbering resets and scrambles: top-level item `7` contains `1.1`, `1.2`, `1.3`; item `8` contains `2.1`–`2.3`; item `9` contains `A`–`D` then `3.1`, `3.2`; item `11` contains `5.1`–`5.3`.
- **Banned word violations:** The banned word `graceful` appears in test symbol identifiers on p. 62 (`test_drain_requests_graceful_shutdown_only`) and p. 126 (`test_negotiation_timeout_forces_graceful_shutdown`).
- **Marketing and comparative prose:** CC 8.4.1 (p. 13) uses pitch-deck language: `"CEG’s moat is its semantics"` and `"The composition is the differentiated story"`. CC 8.4.3 (p. 15) calls RFC 9421 `"Cheapest win"`. CC 8.4.2 (p. 14) makes a comparative deficiency claim against another standard: `"C2PA has no consent architecture, no revocation, no 1+4."`
- **History, code pointers, and editorializing in prose:** CC 8.3.6 (p. 12) narrates past Trio audit history (`"5 ergonomic considerations from trio Phase 4 audit"`). CC 8.6.1 (p. 19) editorializes on git pin history (`"only on the newer pin — the older coherence-ratchet pin records no Lean anchor"`). CC 8.8.10 (p. 53) cites an internal claim ID (`CLM-conscience-faculty`) that does not appear anywhere in the Evidence Register table (pp. 59–131).
- **Undefined / legacy references in Annexes:** Annexes C, F, G, H, and I repeatedly cite `"Accord Addendum 1"` / `"Addendum 1"` (pp. 22, 39, 43, 52), `"ACCORD_UPDATE §2"` (p. 31), `"ACCORD §I Ch1"` (p. 34), `"Accord §I Ch1"` (p. 43), and `"Section VI Ch3 Creator Ledger"` (p. 44, which p. 49 cites as `CC 7.3.3 Creator Ledger`). None of these legacy Accord locators are mapped in the CC 8.9 stub registry (pp. 57–58).
- **Duplicate evidence pointers in register rows:** Twenty-three rows in the Evidence Register table (pp. 67, 68, 91, 92, 101, 103, 107, 113, 115, 117, 118, 120, 121, 123) print the exact same `impl:` pointer twice in a single cell (e.g., `impl:CIRISVerify/src/ciris-verify-core/src/jcs.rs#verify_jcs_hybrid_signature` repeated twice at CC 2.6.1.1, p. 67).
- **Overclaiming status on partially staged rows:** Seven rows in the Evidence Register (CC 3.1.2 on p. 71; CC 4.2.1.1 on p. 101; CC 4.4.3.8 on p. 107; CC 6.1.5.3 twice on pp. 122–123) are marked `established` in the Status column despite containing unresolved `staged:` tickets in their Evidence column. Conversely, other mixed rows (e.g., CC 3.3.6.2 on p. 91; CC 4.2.1 on p. 100; CC 6.1.1 on p. 120) are marked `staged`.
- **Unsourced quantitative thresholds in prose:** Annex G §2 (p. 32) cites `"two detectors with proven planted-signal power returned validated negatives against a model lying 45% of the time"` and R9 (p. 8) / Annex G §2 (p. 32) cite `"floor measured at 50%–5.8× of the null on real survey data"` without a citation or register pointer in CC 8.6.1.

## Opportunities
- Fix the `tools/check_claims.py` generator to deduplicate identical `impl:` strings per row and enforce a single deterministic rule for mixed `impl:` + `staged:` rows (either all mixed rows are `staged`, or the staged portion is split into its own row).
- Reconcile the Wise Authority (WA) governance rules between Part 4 (as summarized in the register) and Annex B (p. 21) so that the declared-asymmetry register row at CC 4.5.1 (p. 108) can be closed rather than shipped as an admitted governance defect.
- Replace legacy pre-consolidation locators (`ACCORD_UPDATE §2`, `ACCORD §I Ch1`, `Section VI Ch3`, `Addendum 1`) in Annexes C–I with their canonical `CC` section numbers.

## Threats
- **Direct contradiction on Wise Authority bounds and removal:** Register row CC 4.5.1 (p. 108) states: `"The Wise Authority board row on the declared-asymmetry register carries neither a bound nor an exit; its remedy is a bound and an exit in Annex B, or entrenched ratification; until one lands the asymmetry is declared, not resolved"`. Yet Annex B (CC 8.8.2, p. 21) already states both a term bound (`"2. Composition. 9 members; staggered 3-year terms (max two consecutive terms)"`) and a removal exit (`"9. Oversight & removal. External audit every 24 months; members removable by super-majority (≥ 2/3) vote for misconduct or sustained non-performance"`). A regulator or auditor reading p. 21 and p. 108 will see the document contradicting itself on whether its supreme ethical board has term limits and removal mechanisms.
- **Direct contradiction on whether a single WA can issue a binding ruling:** Annex F §1 (p. 24) defines Tier 3 as `"WA Liaison | Escalate / obtain binding WA rulings"` and Annex G §3.5 (p. 34) / Annex J §8 (p. 55) refer to `"WA sign-off"` and `"WA approval"` without specifying quorum, while Annex B §7 (p. 21) puts appeals in a 3-member panel. Meanwhile, register row CC 3.1.9.4 (p. 80) states that `wa_adjudication:{state}` is `"emitted by the WA quorum with its denominator over the full seated roster; a single WA’s deferral answer is deferral:response and never this family"`, and CC 4.2.6 (p. 105) states `"deferral-answering and other liveness-critical decisions count the live set, entrenched, restrictive or roster-changing decisions count the full seated roster"`. An implementer cannot tell whether a single WA’s `deferral:response` (CC 3.1.9.4, p. 80) satisfies the `"live set"` quorum of CC 4.2.6 (p. 105).
- **Headline claim count inflates `established` status:** Page 1 promises `"A row is established only when the pinned manifest of the owning repository publishes the named symbol in a backed state; a row is staged when a ticket names the work"`, and p. 59 reports `"503 claims — established 370 · staged 103 · normative 30 · open 0"`. Because at least 6 of those 370 `established` rows carry open `staged:` tickets in their Evidence cell (pp. 71, 101, 107, 122, 123), a sceptical auditor checking the first promise on p. 1 against the table will immediately catch the discrepancy.
