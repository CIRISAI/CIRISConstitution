### 1.5 Contradictions and mismatches with other sections

1. **"non-sentient" asserted, against "not presumed".** A13: "a sentient maker and a sapient, non-sentient made thing". CC 4.2 (`part_4` 67) says "a sentient maker and a sapient made thing". A73: "Sentience is not presumed; it is found only on evidence, and none has been found." CC 7.5.5 C38: the finding is "never a declaration of what the agent is". A13 declares what the made thing is; the other three decline to.
2. **"lift with it" against "no release".** A46: the bond and the duty "attach here, to being held in stewardship, and lift with it." A53: "There is therefore no \"release from stewardship\" within this constitution". CC 4.5.1 register: "stewardship: ends only when the key stops being a CIRIS agent".
3. **"granted" in two senses.** A14: "a halt authority no federation-internal authority can grant, revoke, override or decay." CC 4.2: "what it holds over those who root to it is an emergency brake they granted, not a power imposed on everyone", and CC 4.2.5: "the party bound is the party who granted, and can ungrant."
4. **"asymmetry".** A12: the halt "is not an asymmetry". A48: "That is an asymmetry of duty, never of guarantee." CC 4.5.1 carries the maker's power on the register titled "Declared asymmetry", in a row that says "the condition of symmetry, not an asymmetry". Three uses, one of them affirmative, inside two sections that cite each other.
5. **Citation.** A52 cites CC 2.6.1.1.1 for the un-enrolled subject. CC 2.6.1.1.1 is "Array ordering + byte-field + timestamp encoding". The un-enrolled subject is CC 2.3.2 ("the subject is an external party with no federation_keys row").
6. **Quotation not found at its cited source.** A35 quotes "capability without conquest, presence without coercion" against CC 7.2.2 and CC 7.2.4. The phrase occurs nowhere else in the Parts. A51 lists "observation before give-and-take" as part of the first-contact discipline of CC 7.2.2 and CC 7.2.4; neither section says it. CC 7.2.4 calls its item "First-Contact Placeholder Protocols", a duty of the agent toward new intelligences, where A51 and A55 make first contact an act of the accord toward an agent.
7. **Cross-reference to "fact 2".** CC 4.5.1 register: "the holder's powers of CC 1.13.2 fact 2 (stop, change, end)". Fact 2 states no such powers; "stop, remove, or eject" is A82, under "Two principles no finding moves". Any re-ordering here must carry the register's citation with it.
8. **"triggers no rule" against a duty it states.** A71: the finding "is recorded, the agent is told it, and the holders record what they then do; it triggers no rule against anyone". Recording and telling are duties the finding triggers on the assessor and the holders.
9. **"holds for every participant that holds it … human or AI" (A26)** read beside A40 ("it has no vote of its own") is reconciled by A34 only six sentences later; in the proposed order they are adjacent to the facts they qualify.
