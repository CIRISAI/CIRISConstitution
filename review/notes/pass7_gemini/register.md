# The Evidence Register (ciris_constitution-1.0-rc12-register.pdf, 132 pages)

**Care score:** 3
**One-line verdict:** A rigorous and remarkably candid evidence-mapping volume that needs a targeted cleanup pass to purge internal Contradictions on Wise Authority terms, unbacked empirical numbers in prose, and leftover audit/ticket jargon in Part 8.

## Strengths
- **Explicit epistemic grading of evidence (pp. 18–19):** CC 8.6.1 separates artifact existence (`impl`, `test`, `lean`, `bench`) from epistemic force (`identity`, `theorem-given-model`, `measured`, `cited-external`), explicitly warning readers that a `lean:` pointer does not prove an empirical claim about the world.
- **Candid concerns and negative-result disclosure (pp. 6–12, 53–54):** CC 8.3 ("Concerns + acknowledged gaps") and CC 8.8.10 ("Annex J") state outright what the system cannot do—such as private-content CSAM detection under E2EE (p. 8) and untuned single-turn harm where a plain prompt outperformed the full pipeline (p. 54).
- **Zero open stubs and machine-checked spine (pp. 58–59):** CC 8.9 confirms 0 open stubs, and the 507-row Evidence Register table (pp. 59–132) cleanly separates `established` (370), `staged` (107), and `normative` (30) claims.

## Weaknesses
- **Internal contradiction on Wise Authority removal threshold (p. 22 vs. p. 108):** Annex B §9 states WA members are "removable by super-majority (≥ 2/3) vote" (p. 22), and the register row for 4.5.1 (p. 108) repeats "removal by a two-thirds vote, §9". However, the register row for 4.2.6 (p. 105) rules that for a WA body, "entrenched, restrictive or roster-changing decisions count the full seated roster, and a decision that unseats a member is tallied at the pre-change denominator" without specifying 2/3, while CC 4.2.6 in other volumes / rows (e.g., p. 105) uses strict majority of the standing roster for roster changes. More glaringly, Annex B §2 (p. 21) sets "staggered 3-year terms (max two consecutive terms)" and p. 108 calls this an "operational target and not a wire object", whereas CC 4.4.3.2.8 (p. 107) mandates that a `delegates_to` carrying `review` or `slash` "MUST carry delegation_valid_until at most 365 days... the ceiling is constitutional".
- **Unbacked empirical numbers quoted in prose (pp. 7, 33):** Both R9 (p. 7) and the TX-9 measurement caveat in Annex G (p. 33) quote specific empirical figures while admitting they have no register backing: "measured at 50%–5.8× of the null on real survey data; measured, not yet a register row" and "two detectors with proven planted-signal power returned validated negatives against a model lying 45% of the time (measured; not yet a register row)".
- **Orphaned pointer in Annex J prose (p. 53):** CC 8.8.10 states: "The registry row is CLM-conscience-faculty (CC 8.8.10)." No row named `CLM-conscience-faculty` appears in the printed Evidence Register; the sole row for 8.8.10 on p. 131 has no `CLM-` identifier printed and is `staged:CIRISConstitution#88`. (Similarly, `CLM-symbol` is referenced on p. 119 under 5.4.2 without a printed `CLM-` ID column in the table.)
- **Comparative claims about external projects and payment rails (pp. 6, 13, 16):** R1 (p. 6) bets that "earned-Credits-weighting still outperforms token-weighting at scale"; CC 8.4.1 (p. 13) names "AP2 / Visa TAP / Mastercard Agent Pay" as "vendor rails / competing semantic layers"; CC 8.4.3 (p. 16) contrasts CEG against IETF SCITT profiles ("Where a SCITT profile mandates ES256 and defers post-quantum signing, the CEG interior already carries the ML-DSA-65 half").
- **History, release-archaeology, and ticket references in Part 8 prose and claim descriptions (pp. 11, 88, 96, 116):** While the Evidence column legitimately cites tickets and code, the prose and Claim column leak history and internal jargon: "Five ergonomic considerations raised in audit" (p. 11), "the trace-capture grant CIRISAgent has emitted since 2.9.6" (p. 88), "(the repairable_does_not_factor adoption, verify#248)" and "(the lake’s binding-fusion correction)" (p. 96), "(OQ-1)... (OQ-2)... (OQ-3)" (p. 97), and "the length-disambiguated v3.7.0 compatibility rule is withdrawn" (p. 116).
- **Undefined or dangling placeholder references (pp. 3, 24):** Page 3 cites "(retain / legal-hold, CC 3.x archive_mode)" with a literal `3.x` placeholder. Page 24 cites "per the Introduction’s Liability section, compliance claims are void where prohibited by applicable law"—an Introduction that does not exist in this volume.
- **Normative RFC 2119 keywords inside an explicitly informative section (pp. 15, 74):** CC 8.4 opens with a box stating "This whole section is informative (CC 2.5)" (p. 12), yet CC 8.4.2.2 (p. 15) uses "MAY" and "does NOT", and register row 8.4.2.2 (p. 129) says "C2PA emit is normative for generated media". Similarly, row 3.1.4 (p. 74) asserts "registry 149 families / 968 vectors" in prose without showing the source of those exact counts in this volume.

## Opportunities
- **Print the Claim ID (`CLM-*`) column in the Evidence Register table (pp. 59–132) or strip `CLM-*` prose references (pp. 53, 119):** Printing the stable claim ID or removing the two prose references (`CLM-conscience-faculty`, `CLM-symbol`) will eliminate dangling internal identifiers.
- **Promote the two "not yet a register row" measurements (pp. 7, 33) into `staged` rows in `claims.tsv`:** Creating staged rows for the survey-data manufactured floor (50%–5.8×) and the 45%-lying detector negative result will bring the volume into 100% compliance with the house rule that every empirical claim in prose is backed by a register row.
- **Scrub internal shorthand from the Claim column of `claims.tsv` (pp. 88, 96, 97, 116):** A 30-minute pass over `claims.tsv` to remove `(verify#248)`, `(the lake's binding-fusion correction)`, `(OQ-1..3)`, `since 2.9.6`, and `v3.7.0` will make the register self-contained for an external auditor.

## Threats
- **Present-tense claims on the cover page vs. 107 staged rows in the register (p. 1 vs. p. 59):** Page 1 promises "That present tense is used only for what ships." However, the Claim column for all 107 `staged` rows (e.g., 1.2 on p. 59, 2.4 on p. 63, 3.1.3 on p. 72, 3.1.4 on p. 74, 5.4.6 on pp. 120–121) is written in unconditional present tense ("Every prefix admitted to the namespace passes...", "The row-type slot is closed...", "An onion packet is fixed-length..."), indistinguishable in phrasing from `established` rows. A hostile auditor or regulator reading the Claim text without cross-checking the Status column will accuse the document of violating its own page-1 promise.
- **Annexes F–J read as operational reality rather than targets (pp. 21–57):** Although CC 8.8 (p. 21) disclaimers that "The annexes are the Accord’s operational targets... They are not evidenced in the register unless a row says so", Annexes F, G, H, I, and J use unconditional present-tense operational assertions ("All alerts surface on DRIFT-∆ Grafana board and page Tier-1 Operator", p. 39; "Dashboard JSON at `/compliance/kpi.json` auto-publishes on each L-Check close", p. 39; "Bot: the regulatory-watch job runs daily", p. 50; "The harness is built around the 'Ethics Engine Enterprise API'", p. 55). An implementer or regulator could easily read these as shipped infrastructure rather than aspirational deployment targets.
- **Banned-word leakage in test symbol names (pp. 62, 127):** While prose obeys the banned-word list, the banned word `graceful` appears twice in printed test function identifiers in the Evidence column (`test_drain_requests_graceful_shutdown_only` on p. 62; `test_negotiation_timeout_forces_graceful_shutdown` on p. 127).
