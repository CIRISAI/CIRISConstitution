## Reading the three passes together

Pass 1 found rendering. Pass 2, with the rendering fixed, found the content contradictions the renderer had hidden, and R25–R40 closed most of them. Pass 3, from a model that did not write the text, found a second layer of the same kind — older design sentences left beside the ruled ones (the halt in root validity, the live-set fire, the conditional at-rest branch, the GPA claim for the Anonymous Tier) — and a residue class the checker does not match (ticket ids, story counts, attributions, external studies in tables). The next pass should run after R41–R56 and M1–M8 land, on a different family again, and should be told the model's training cutoff relative to the citations it will meet.
