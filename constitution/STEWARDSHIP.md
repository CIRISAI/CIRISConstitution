# Stewardship

Stewarded by **Eric Moore**, founder.

This constitution carries no ratification date and no expiry. It is perpetually incomplete — it
simply *is*: a living document, never finished and never lapsed.

It may be amended by the founder, or by any accord-holder, as the work requires — a
benevolent-steward model in the lineage of the open-source projects this ultimately is: the
maintainer decides, the work stays forkable under its license, and no one is bound who does not
voluntarily join. The document's own governance tightens this over time, never loosens it: once the
mesh reaches scale — the scale gate: ≥100,000 production agents active in a 24-hour period, QA and harness agents excluded — founder authority is *superseded* by
the mechanized amendment process (federation Contribution, Wise-Authority quorum, and the entrenched
M-of-N accord ratification; see CC 4.5.1). Until then, that authority is deferred to mechanization but
exercised by the steward. This is authority over the text of this instance, not over any participant: the steward holds no vote, reach or exemption another participant lacks, and the office is named here, ends at the scale gate, and is left by forking the text or by instantiating the form with your own occupants.
