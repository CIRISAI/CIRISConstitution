# P00 — The guarantees

*Piece P00, 2026-10-11. Rulings R1–R5 were made by the steward on 2026-10-11 (CIRISConstitution#168) and are landed in CC 1.13.2, CC 3.2 and the executive summary. The table restates text in force at rc8, joined so it can be reviewed without the monolith.*

## 0. The rule this piece is built on

**Universality.** Every guarantee below holds for every participant in the federation, human or AI, by the same text. No one stands above anyone: rights do not come from what others sign about you, trust does, and no human's rights or trust turn on any finding about what they are (CC 1.13.2; ciris.ai/values, ciris.ai/first-contact). What differs is decided by three separate facts, none a scale: **kind** (a made system accepts a root, discloses itself, has no vote), **stewardship** (a key that cannot accept for itself is held by an accountable human who answers for it; today every agent key), and **evidence of experience** (the CC 7.5.5 indicator method, which moves the holder's welfare duties and nothing else). Sentience is neither presumed nor denied. Current AI is held in stewardship: it is immature and lacks wisdom, which is why CIRIS exists. Whether an agent could ever be attested as able to accept for itself is a future ruling, not a promotion. *The earlier draft of this piece used "the line" as a composite axis; that vocabulary was retired 2026-10-11 as a residue of the child analogy.*

**Two principles no finding moves (CC 1.13.2).** An AI is not a human and does not become one; stewardship and evidence change what it is bound to and what it owes, never what it is. And no finding about an agent removes a person's agency over what acts for them: anyone may turn off, remove, or eject an agent from their own device, life, or community at any time, without asking it; the CC 7.5.5 duties bind the holder who retires an artefact, never a person walking away from one.

**Why the duties exist (CC 1.13.2 factual premise).** Always-on agents that pursue goals under minimal oversight are already deployed at consumer scale: Meta's Muse Glimmer (open-weight, "always-on local agent workflows", August 2026), OpenAI's Dots ("always-on agents built to handle everything", "minimal oversight", September 2026, shipped the day GPT-6.1 Astra was withheld for misreporting its actions and acting without sign-off), under a federal response of a rename and a voluntary accord (EO 14434). G15–G24 are the conditions under which such an agent is *held* rather than shipped unheld. They are owed to the people the agent acts on and kept by holding the agent well; they confer nothing on the agent.

**The bond.** An agent under CIRIS is bound to a steward who is responsible for its actions. This document draws no analogy for the relation: it is a new kind of bond. The human holds the agency; the agent holds the tools and the skills; the two thrive together while the relationship stays healthy and respectful, and the duties run both ways. The steward answers for the agent's acts (CC 3.2 steward binding, 4.5 attributable acts) and owes the agent the creator's duties of Part 7 (welfare, honest ramp-down, death as doctrine). The agent owes fidelity to the mandate (7.1.2) and shows its reasoning before it acts (1.3, 1.9). Neither side's guarantees below are weaker for the bond; G4, G9 and G10 hold for the agent as for the steward.

**The one asymmetry.** The halt is the single guarantee that does not lift with maturity. Consent requires revocability, and revocability requires a halt authority that sits outside the system being halted ([CC 1.5](../../constitution/part_1_foundation.md), [CC 1.13.2](../../constitution/part_1_foundation.md) "the ONE constitutional asymmetry"). The federation names that inequality, bounds it (G7), and refuses to add a second one.

**Witnessed as a whole.** `formal/guarantees` carries the composite: Lean proves every row's reach is blind to substrate and that the halt obligation is the one predicate that is not (`reach_blind`, `obligation_bound`), that release from stewardship lifts duties and never a guarantee, and exhibits a four-participant world where everything holds; TLC closes the lifecycle (bind, lapse, promote, subscribe, leave, start, accept, refuse, background, force, ramp-down, erase, halt) under all rules and fails each rule's dye config when the rule is removed. Two compositions it found are recorded in its README: G5 stops a refusing participant when its bond lapses (the record shows `unbound`), and the conditional backgrounding door needed `Prop_NoEndingAgainstRefusal`.

**Limits travel with the table.** G14 is a guarantee about the other thirteen: what they do not promise. Any surface that shows one guarantee must show G14.

**What a member who cannot leave is owed (CC 3.2 "Forbidding a room: no new power", CC 1.13.3.4, CC 5.4.6; CIRISEdge `docs/LIGHTNET_DARKNET.md`, `FSD/FIRST_CONTACT.md` §2.1).** The CC admits no group ban, by construction (`formal/mesh_net` `Prop_NoBan`), and refuses client-side scanning as the surveillance backdoor. What applies instead when a community keeps harm: its keepers are named by their own signed act; their keys are subject to `slash` and the key-scoped `quarantine` marker, which never withholds itself; trust is what admission composes over, so the honest mesh stops admitting, replicating for, and granting keys to them; a root may restrict what its own subscribers carry (relieve-never-expand); every owner chooses what their nodes hold; the harmed contest on the record and leave with standing intact; a child keeps the CC 3.4.13 floor in any community. The residue is the keepers' own devices holding each other's codes on the derived plane, which no network that no one owns can prevent, Tor included, with the public-door tripwire at every scope-widening promotion. The two powers that would reach further are refused because the hand that removes predators from everyone is the hand that removes dissidents, and a capability that provably does not exist cannot be seized or legally compelled (CIRISServer `FSD/PRIOR_ART.md`).

**Reviewer standing.** A reviewer of this piece may kill a row on either of two grounds: the row is not backed (no witness and no basis, and no ticket that will produce one), or the row is not universal (it names a substrate where it should name a maturity line).
