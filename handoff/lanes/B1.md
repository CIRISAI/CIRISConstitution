# Lane B1: sentences that carry more than their rule's home

Read COMMON.md first, then `<scratch>/settle/BRIEF.md`
(the verdicts and the repair format are the same).

`formal/derivation/consolidation_findings.tsv` lists 39 sentences: each was marked as restating
a rule, and a reader found it carries a condition, party or bound its rule's home lacks
(`EXTRA`), or differs from the home (`CONFLICT`). Columns: section, verdict, home, sentence,
what_differs.

For each `EXTRA`: read the sentence in its section and the home. Decide:
- `MOVE`: the extra belongs to the rule and the home should state it. Give the repair on the
  HOME (`file`, `old`, `new`: one added sentence or clause, under 40 words, in the home's own
  register), and a second repair on the original sentence only if it then needs a citation
  (`cite_old`, `cite_new`: the same sentence with " ([CC home](link))" before its final stop;
  never inside a fence or a table row).
- `LOCAL`: the extra is the section's own application of the rule to its subject (a family's
  own bound, an example with force for that family only). It stays; the inventory's `role`
  should be `applies`, not `restates`. No repair.
- `STALE`: the extra contradicts the home and the home is right. Repair the sentence.
For each `CONFLICT`: use the settle brief's verdicts.

Output JSON at the path your launch message gives: a list of
`{"n" (row number in the findings file, 1-based without the header), "section", "home",
"verdict", "reason" (under 50 words), "file", "old", "new", "cite_file", "cite_old",
"cite_new", "changes_rule", "confidence"}`. Check by script that every non-empty `old` and
`cite_old` occurs exactly once in its file.

Final message: counts per verdict and the three you were least sure of. Under 120 words.
