**The trust root, ruled before it is built (#118–#121 → CC 3.2 T4a, T6, T7, T8; 2026-09-28).** Four rules land in CC 3.2's trust-root operational semantics, each anchored on the ticket that implements it, none touching T4's *valid until revoked*. **T4a — attaching is gated on freshness; attached is not.** A consumer that attaches or re-roots MUST hold the root's lineage head — the latest roster-chain record, named by the SHA-256 of its JCS-canonical signed bytes and its signer-stamped `asserted_at` — witnessed under T6 and no older than the charter's `attach_window` under the CC 2.6.7 skew rule; a stale or unwitnessed head is insufficient to attach (`trust_root_head_stale` at the acceptance-edge door), never invalid, and a lapsed window never detaches a running node. `attach_window` and `witness_cadence` are charter members, changed by re-scrub only; the shipped defaults for `ciris-canonical` and `humanity-accord` are 7 days and 24 hours (CC 5.3.4). TUF's expiries close the same hole for a repository and do not transplant whole, because an expiring root lets one dead timer un-federate every honest node at once. **T6 — a trust-root lineage is witnessed.** Every roster-chain head of a conferring family or `infrastructure` community is cosigned on the CC 5.3.1 witness plane by witnesses that are not its founders, re-committed at least once per cadence whether or not the head moved; two witnessed heads for one lineage are `hard_case:lineage_equivocation`, neither adopted, adjudicated as ledgers are; an unwitnessed competing head is refused, and first-seen-wins is retired for conferring lineages. CC 5.3.1 gains the object — `POST /v1/transparency/lineage-head/cosign` under `ciris.lineage_head_cosign.v1`, a domain label and byte layout distinct from the STH cosign so neither replays as the other, with descent verified from the witness's prior head and a founder-signed non-descendant head *recorded uncosigned* as the evidence rather than refused (`LINEAGE_HEAD_INCONSISTENT` is the malformed case only); CC 5.3.4 serves the witnessed head beside the bundle and alone at `GET /v1/trust-root/lineage-head`; CC 4.4's two cold-start sentences gain the head; Part I's adversary table gains the equivocating founder. **T7 — liveness margin and the stalled state.** A trust-root-grade `quorum:M/N` collective is live at ≥ M + 1 active founders (resignations, `withdraws`, `revoked_after` and every `node`-bearing seat removed) and stalled at ≤ M, where every remaining founder is a veto — the state persist's review named and the absolute-M reading creates after the first exit at N = 3. Stalled is declared (`hard_case:community_liveness_stalled` / `_restored`), valid but non-admitting, T4 holding; recovery is the conferring body's, never a lowering of M (entrenchment) and never the members'; founding at N = M is non-conformant for `infrastructure`, and a floor that raises the effective M (CIRISPersist#927 ask 2) is refused at admission rather than applied silently. The accord family stays under CC 4.2.6's live-set rule; CC 4.4.3.4.2.1 says in one paragraph why absolute-M and the margin are different rules. **T8 — the standing verdict is a state machine.** `Rooted` / `Stalled` / `NotRooted` over the roster chain and its witnessed head, resignations, `withdraws`/`recants`/`revoked_after`, every seat's `identity_type`, the node's own acceptance edges, the halt latch and signer-stamped instants; every transition a row that exists today; seven invariants a model MUST prove, including that no `node`-bearing seat and no lapsed timer ever moves a state, that a resignation never reaches behind the head's instant, and that the verdict is deterministic in rows × instant. The evidence is a mechanized model (TLA+ or Alloy) that first encodes the five rc5 review corners as named invariants — the model begins by proving it would have caught what the reviewers caught. Rows: `CLM-attach-freshness` and `CLM-witnessed-lineage` staged on CIRISPersist#937 / #938 and CIRISServer#693, `CLM-lineage-head-cosign` (5.3.1) on CIRISServer#693, `CLM-liveness-margin` on CIRISPersist#939, `CLM-standing-verdict` on this repository's #127. The four ruling issues close when this text reaches `main`; their rows' anchors are the implementing tickets, never the rulings.

**Three-reviewer round on the trust-root rulings — twenty-eight findings, one revision (2026-09-28).** A CT/TUF security reviewer, a consensus-liveness and formal-methods reviewer, and the persist implementer read T4a/T6/T7/T8 and the 5.3.1/5.3.4 text independently; they converged on five defects and the text now reads as follows. **Freshness is the witnesses' instant.** The first cut measured `attach_window` against the head's own `asserted_at`, which never moves on a stable roster, so every honest root would have become unattachable after seven days; T4a now measures the newest *counted* cosignature's `signed_at` (TUF puts the expiry on the timestamp role, not on targets), requires `attach_window_secs > witness_cadence_secs + skew`, and names the rollback exposure at attach as exactly the window. **The head is an object.** Persist's rosters are a base row plus separately scrub-signed widening, revocation and charter rows folded at read — no `supersedes` chain exists to descend by — so T6 defines `lineage_head` (`ciris.lineage_head.v1`: previous digest, roster-fold digest, charter digest, `asserted_at`, signed by the lineage's own conferring quorum), issued on every roster-affecting row and re-issued per cadence; the accord family's head is the object its next `accord_proposal` names as prior; the genesis heads ride inside `bundle`. **Witnessed has a number and a directory the attach door can use.** `witness_quorum` (≥ 2, a strict majority of the directory, so two witnessed heads force one witness to double-sign attributably) and `witnesses[]` are charter members inside the scrub-signed bytes, hence inside the bundle and pinned by its fingerprint — a node holding no root yet verifies witnesses without the delegation walk persist's `witness` identity would otherwise require; independence excludes founders, member seats and anything owner- or steward-bound to a founder (a `ciris-canonical` serve node does not witness `ciris-canonical`), evaluated at `signed_at`; a directory change is witnessed by the directory before it. **Two evidence tiers, and the freeze is narrow.** Only two heads each meeting the quorum are `lineage_equivocation` and freeze head adoption; a quorum-signed non-descendant is `lineage_fork_attempt`, recorded once per fork point, never cosigned, never freezing, so a revoked founder set cannot freeze the honest lineage by signing forks forever; the freeze never suspends `withdraws`, `recants`, tombstones, `revoked_after` or the halt, so an equivocating key cannot hold its own revocation hostage. **T7's recovery edge is admissible.** The first cut said recovery was "the conferring body's" and named no row a stalled roster could admit — the ceremony plane confers identity on a key and does not change a founder list. T7 now admits exactly one row without the founders' count: a widening adding a founder seat for a key bearing a live ceremony-plane conferral from the conferring family, verified on the conferral, bounded like H7 (add only, human only, while stalled only, never removes, never changes M), which removes the veto #120 named rather than relocating it. Also from the round: `humanity-accord` is excluded from T7 by name (CC 4.2.6 governs it) and T8 evaluates the margin over the root only; "conferring family" is defined (a family a live charter names as root); the active-founder predicate is stated as a formula, `identity_type` is read at seating (a later `node` is a resignation at that instant), `M ≥ 2`, "to M or fewer"; *non-admitting* is defined per door; the stalled/restored and equivocation emitters are named with `event_id` derivations so N nodes do not emit N events; the acceptance edge carries `attached_head_digest` and is write-gated with two tokens (`trust_root_head_stale`, `trust_root_head_unwitnessed`); the cosign preimage carries the prior digest and the route accepts an equal head as a re-commitment and bounds `signed_at` to ±5 min of receipt; charter members are integers in seconds; T8 is stated as V(rows, t) over signer-stamped t with the skew rule confined to the attach refusal, `Rooted` gains the scope and custody legs persist already enforces, `NotRooted` is the exhaustive "otherwise", transitions include instant crossings, and invariant (viii) says what a lowered `revoked_after` does (read-time, fail-closed, the one input that moves the verdict backward, by design). The `quorum:{m}/{n}` row at CC 3.2 no longer says `n` is the current roster size. The five implementing tickets carry the deltas as comments.

**Second pass by the same reviewers — twelve findings, twelve fixes.** The cadence duty moves to the **witnesses**: the first revision told the lineage to re-issue its head every cadence, which is a daily founder ceremony wearing a different hat and contradicts 5.3.1's "equality is the re-commitment"; a head object now changes only when the fold or charter changes, each witness re-cosigns the unchanged head per cadence, and the rollback exposure at attach is the window plus skew, not "exactly" the window. `witness_quorum` is **derived**, ⌊|witnesses|/2⌋ + 1 — the shipped "2 over at least three" was not a strict majority of four and broke the double-signer argument; the default is exactly three witnesses. The accord family issues `lineage_head` objects too, and `prior_family_digest` names the current one, so it no longer has two head definitions. A fork point is the nearest ancestor **on the witnessed chain**, so a chain of forks off a fork cannot escape the once-per-point storage bound. Cosignatures verify against the **parent's** directory, never the head's own `charter_digest` (which a fork head chooses), and the route serves the head chain since genesis with folds and charters fetchable by digest, because a verifier cannot walk descent on a current head alone. Directory rotation gains its recovery edge: when the previous directory cannot reach its own quorum, the conferring body re-seats it by charter re-scrub, recorded as `hard_case:lineage_directory_reseated`, and that head is attachable only through an out-of-band anchor. The T4a write gate runs on the edge's first admission only, so a node's own restore never refuses its own root. On T7: the recovery widening's conferral MUST name the collective, is signed by the conferred key, and the **residual is named** — a conferring body that can both retire and confer founders can replace a stalled roster seat by seat, which is the power that founded it; the membership-ceremony sentence that said the door widens only by the existing door's consent is amended to say so. On T8: `Rooted` drops "not frozen" (the freeze is attach-time only, and a history-dependent leg broke determinism) and drops holder custody (an attestation chain can lapse without a row; custody is a seating gate); the halt is an input *as it reaches this node*; an edge naming a head that (viii) un-heads is read as naming the nearest verifying ancestor; (ii), (iii), (iv), (vi), (vii) and (viii) are restated in checkable form — V constant on row-free intervals, `withdraws` never reaching behind while revocation reaches behind only through its bound, edge-written as a guarded transition, re-entry to `Rooted` without a new edge forbidden only from the no-edge case, determinism as fold commutativity, and (viii) as an equation over rows with the un-headed subtree removed. The model at `formal/trust_root/` encodes the machine as revised.

**Erratum: a key revocation names its subject by the key's digest (#134; CC 2.4.1.1, 2.1).** A `key_id` carries the holder's keystore label in cleartext, and revocations replicate federation-wide, so every node learned labels such as `frank-laptop` from revocations of keys it did not hold. **Reachability, liveness and the senses of "witness", deconflicted (#137, CIRISPersist#974; CC 3.1.9.4, 3.2 T6, 3.4.10, 3.1.3).** Four things did not agree. (1) The text said ciris-status emits `health:liveness`; it never has, by design — its targets mostly have no key, and persist's rule for that family is that a service never attests its own liveness. What it emits, `observation:reachability`, was unregistered. It is now registered as a **first-person** probe result (signer = subject, `witness_relation: self`, the target named by a label in `observed`), `health:liveness` is restated as the third-person claim about a keyed service (signer ≠ subject), `peer_reachability:{network}` is named as the edge's own substrate report, and CC 3.3.7's example is corrected. The `observation:` stem is closed to the one leaf.
