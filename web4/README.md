# CIRIS Web4 Constitutional Mesh

*CIRIS Web4 Constitutional Mesh* is a white paper: constitutional governance of a decentralized mesh. It sets out the case for the constitutional mesh, what it promises a person, how the causal kernel and the mesh keep those promises, and the evidence for each claim, marked Implemented, Specified or Research thesis. It stands beside the Constitution and makes no rule; where the two differ, the Constitution governs. Its plain-language companion is [`../VISION.md`](../VISION.md).
