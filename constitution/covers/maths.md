# The Mathematics

**What this is.** Part 6, *The Substrate*: the canonicalisation boundary and the 1+4 line, the census, replication and the wholeness witness, the noise-floor model, the effective source count that gates aggregation, and the rules of the substrate stated with the arguments behind them.

**Whom it binds.** The implementer of a substrate: its census, replication, witness and aggregation functions are rules here, stated with RFC 2119 force, beside the arguments for them. A theorem among them binds by being true; the rules bind by conformance, like the rules of *The Wire*. The volume names the places where an argument is weaker than its statement.

**What it promises.** The substrate's rules with the arguments behind them: the noise floor as a checkable predicate, the effective count stated with its clamp, its ceiling and its exposed direction, the descent and replication rules, and the census. Two results carry mechanised witnesses, the effective-count discount and the census ballot model, and the register pins them; where an argument stands without a model, the text says so.

**What it leaves out.** The engineering: codec choices, chunk sizes and endpoint shapes are in *The Wire*. The ethics the mathematics serves are in *The Charter*.

**Where the rest lives.** The two witnesses are named in the register, which pins the repositories they live in. A reviewer who wants to kill a result should start from the register row, not from the prose.
